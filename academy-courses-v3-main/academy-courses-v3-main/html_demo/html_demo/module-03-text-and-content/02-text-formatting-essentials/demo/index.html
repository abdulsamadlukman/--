Mama Comfort's Jollof Rice

This is Mama Comfort's famous jollof rice recipe — rich, smoky, and full of flavor, considered one of the best rice dishes in the world1.
Serves 4 people.

The dish's name, jollof, is believed to come from the Wolof people of Senegal.

Ingredients

This recipe uses Nigerian long-grain parboiled rice, though any long-grain rice works.
