Aunty Bisi's Moin Moin

This is Aunty Bisi's classic moin moin recipe — a steamed bean pudding, popular across Nigeria1.
Serves 6 people.

"Moin moin is proof that the best things take patience."

The name, moin moin, comes from the sound of the pot bubbling as it steams.

Ingredients

This recipe uses black-eyed peas, though honey beans work too.

  • Black-eyed peas
  • Onions
  • Red pepper
  • Palm oil
  • Seasoning
    • Stock cubes
    • Salt
  • Fillings
    • Boiled eggs
    • Fish
    • Corned beef

Instructions

  1. Soak and peel the beans
  2. Blend the beans with onions and pepper
  3. Whisk in the palm oil
  4. Pour into wrapped moulds with your chosen fillings
  5. Steam for 45 minutes

Tips

Don't skip the whisking step — it's what makes the pudding light, not dense.

Storage

Keeps in the fridge for 3 days 4 days.
