Our Story

Blessing started baking out of her kitchen in 2019, selling bread to neighbours on her street. Word spread quickly — within a year, she had opened a small shop on Herbert Macaulay Way, and it's been growing ever since. Everything is still made the same way it was in that first kitchen: fresh ingredients, no shortcuts, and a lot of patience.

We now supply bread to three schools nearby, cater for weekend parties, and open every day except Sunday. If you've never had our meat pie, that's the one to start with.
